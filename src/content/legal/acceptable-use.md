Writey turns typed or pasted text into handwriting-style pages for preview and PDF/PNG export. This page explains how you may use that tool, and what we will not build.

## 1. Allowed uses

You may use Writey to create handwriting-style pages for:

- Personal notes and organisation
- Revision and study materials for your own learning
- Letters and journals
- Accessibility needs (for example, when handwriting at length is difficult and you still want a handwritten look)
- Teacher or classroom examples, practice demonstrations, or sample notes, where that is allowed by your institution

These are the uses we design for and talk about on the site.

## 2. Academic integrity

You are responsible for how you use anything you export.

You must not use Writey to engage in academic dishonesty. That includes submitting generated pages as if they were your own handwritten assignment, practical file, or project file when doing so would break your school or college rules, or any applicable examination rule.

We know that many people find this category of tool by searching for ways to turn typed or ChatGPT text into pages that look handwritten for submission. That market fact does not make that use an allowed use, and we do not promote it. Legal, honest uses exist; they are what the product is positioned for.

If your institution requires work to be written by hand by you, you must follow that rule. Writey does not authorise a shortcut around it.

## 3. Features we will not build

We will not offer, sell, or enable:

- **Anti-Copy / Anti-OCR** interference, including textures or patterns whose purpose is to disrupt recognition
- **Auto Mistake** features, including automatic “human” errors, fake strike-throughs, or an adjustable error rate (ordinary aesthetic ink variation is not this)
- **Scan disguise**, including fake scan shadows, flash, folds, or stains used to imitate a scanned sheet
- Any other function or marketing claim whose purpose is that a page “won’t get caught”, is “undetectable”, or will “bypass” a teacher, OCR, or detection tool

Watermarks on the Free plan are ordinary brand watermarks. They are not a scan-disguise effect.

## 4. Other prohibited uses

You also may not:

- Try to bypass plan limits, guest quota, rate limits, or payment (including automated bulk export that evades caps)
- Probe, disrupt, or overload the service, or attempt unauthorised access
- Use the editor to generate or distribute unlawful, infringing, or harmful content
- Extract or redistribute our font files, paper textures, or brand assets except as they appear on pages you export for your own allowed use
- Suggest that Writey is an official school, board, or government service, or that we guarantee marks or results
- Resell the service, or present Waitlist or Contact access as a purchased team licence

## 5. What we may do

If we reasonably believe these rules are being broken, we may limit previews or exports, request a verification challenge, suspend an account, or refuse a discretionary refund associated with abuse. We do not review every page, and enforcement does not make us a party to your academic record.

To report misuse, write to `support@text2handwriting.site`.

## 6. How this sits with the rest of the site

The product, pricing, and marketing must stay consistent with this page. We do not create assignment landing pages, “Create Assignments Now” campaigns, or exam-season passes. The homepage tool remains a text-to-handwriting converter for the allowed uses above.
