## 3. Suggested commercial refund rule (proposal only)

This section is a starting point for Owner review only. It is not in force, and it is not legal advice.

**Proposal:**

1. **Free plan.** There is no payment, so there is nothing to refund.
2. **New Pro subscription.** If you request a refund after the first successful charge for that Pro subscription, and you have not completed a successful Pro export in that period (meaning an export that used a Pro entitlement such as no watermark, high quality, or a Pro-only style), a draft commercial idea is a short unused-window refund of the amount actually paid, through the original payment method where the processor allows it. That window has not been set, and this page does not promise a 7-day refund or any other fixed refund period.
3. **After a Pro export, or after the window.** A change-of-mind refund would not be the default. We would still consider requests in good faith if the service was unavailable or materially did not match the description, without prejudice to any non-waivable rights.
4. **Renewals.** The same unused-window idea may or may not apply to renewals. That choice has not been made. In all cases, you can cancel before the next renewal to avoid a further charge.
5. **Yearly plans.** The same proposed window would be counted from the charge date, not spread across the whole year, unless Owner and counsel decide otherwise. That choice has not been made.
6. **Cancellation without refund.** You may cancel at any time. Access continues until the end of the paid period, then returns to Free, unless a confirmed refund is completed earlier.
7. **How to ask.** Email `support@text2handwriting.site` from the account email, with the approximate date of the charge and, if you have it, the payment-processor reference. Do not send full card numbers, UPI PINs, or one-time passwords.
8. **Timing of payout.** Processor timelines have not been confirmed. We will not invent a “refund in 24 hours” promise.
9. **Taxes.** How GST is reversed or adjusted on a refund has not been confirmed.

Mandatory consumer rights, if any apply to this service and this operator, are **not waived** by this draft. If a confirmed legal review requires a different window or wording, this page will be updated before or when payments go live.

## 4. GST and price display (tax treatment not confirmed)

Writey shows and lists prices in USD, including for users in India (Owner confirmed). Whether GST or other tax applies, whether the operator must register, whether the supply is an electronic service, and whether the advertised USD price must be tax-inclusive are questions of tax law and of the operator’s facts. **This draft does not conclude those questions and does not state a GST rate.** The payment provider has not been named.

Until confirmation:

- Pricing pages and checkout should not mark $3.99, $39, or any other displayed USD price as “incl. GST” or “excl. GST” until tax treatment is confirmed.
- Do not show an unregulated INR equivalent alongside the USD price, and do not treat an exchange-rate snapshot as a fixed front-end conversion.
- Prefer a neutral line such as: “Final charges, including any applicable taxes, will be shown at checkout once tax treatment is confirmed.”
- After confirmation, the live page must state clearly whether the displayed price includes applicable taxes, show the GSTIN if required, and describe how a tax invoice can be obtained.

A direction for Owner review, which is not binding, is: once the rate and registration position are known, prefer consumer-facing prices that already include applicable tax, so the number a person sees is the number they pay. That is a product recommendation, not a legal ruling.

## 5. Payment failures, chargebacks, and abuse

- A failed payment does not create a Pro entitlement.
- If a charge is reversed or a dispute is opened, we may return the account to Free limits and, where reasonable, pause new paid exports until the dispute is resolved.
- We may refuse a discretionary refund, or close an account, if we reasonably believe the request is fraudulent or an attempt to bypass plan limits. This does not block a person from raising a complaint through any forum that remains available under applicable law. Which forums apply has not been settled.
